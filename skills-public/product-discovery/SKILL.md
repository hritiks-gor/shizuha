# Product Discovery

Describe the affected users, their context, the problem and the evidence that it matters. Separate an observed problem from a proposed feature. Define the behavior or outcome to improve and how it will be measured.

Combine interviews, support themes, usage data and direct observation. Note source bias and confidence. Map several plausible solutions and identify the riskiest assumptions for each: usefulness, usability, feasibility and viability.

Validate important assumptions with a small reversible test before committing to a large implementation. State the decision threshold and what evidence would change the plan. Include accessibility, privacy and operational constraints in the comparison.

Create delivery work only with a clear connection to the validated opportunity and observable acceptance criteria. After release, compare the actual outcome with the baseline. If behavior did not improve, revisit the problem and assumptions instead of simply adding more features.
